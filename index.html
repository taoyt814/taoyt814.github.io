简介

你好！我是香港大学机械工程系的博士研究生，隶属自适应机器人控制实验室 (ArcLab)，导师为 陆鹏 (Peng Lu) 教授。

我的研究方向是密集人群中的机器人导航，结合深度强化学习与时空图神经网络 (Spatio-Temporal GNN)，让移动机器人在动态行人环境中实现安全、平滑且符合社会规范的运动。我尤其关注如何对行人交互关系进行建模，并提升导航策略在密集场景下的鲁棒性。

人群导航 深度强化学习 图神经网络 时空建模 机器人运动规划 社会感知导航

About Me

Hi! I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at The University of Hong Kong, working in the Adaptive Robotic Controls Lab (ArcLab) under the supervision of Prof. Peng Lu.

My research focuses on robot navigation in dense crowds, combining deep reinforcement learning with spatio-temporal graph neural networks to achieve safe, smooth, and socially compliant motion among dynamic pedestrians. I am particularly interested in modeling pedestrian interactions and improving the robustness of navigation policies in highly crowded scenes.

Crowd Navigation Deep Reinforcement Learning Graph Neural Networks Spatio-Temporal Modeling Robot Motion Planning Socially-Aware Navigation
